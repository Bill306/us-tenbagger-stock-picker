# Example Case: HIMS Tenbagger Screen

This example shows the intended output style for the `us-tenbagger-stock-picker` skill.

It is an illustrative research example, not investment advice. All numbers, market prices, filings, and operating metrics should be verified with current sources before use.

## Tenbagger Thesis

Hims & Hers Health may be a tenbagger candidate if it continues converting a direct-to-consumer healthcare model into durable free cash flow per share growth.

The bull case is that HIMS is not just an online pharmacy or telehealth provider. It may be building a verticalized healthcare access platform where customer acquisition, online diagnosis, prescription fulfillment, subscription retention, and brand trust reinforce each other.

The main question is whether this model can scale with strong retention and improving per-share economics, rather than relying on temporary product cycles or paid marketing.

Interpretation: Promising but needs more evidence.

## Model Classification

Hybrid model:

- Primary: Costco model
- Secondary: FCF inflection model
- Minor: Data/AI flywheel

HIMS is a better fit for the Costco model than the NVDA model. The thesis depends on scale creating better customer value, better access, stronger retention, and improving free cash flow.

## What The Market May Be Missing

The market may be underestimating HIMS if it views the company as a narrow telehealth brand rather than a vertically integrated consumer healthcare platform.

Potential misunderstanding:

```text
Online health brand -> subscription healthcare platform
```

If customer retention remains high and fulfillment economics improve, the company could grow into a larger healthcare access layer with stronger cash generation than the market expects.

## Key Metrics To Verify

Before relying on this thesis, verify:

- Revenue growth by product category
- Active subscribers
- Monthly online revenue per average subscriber
- Customer retention and repeat purchase behavior
- Gross margin and contribution margin
- Sales and marketing as a percentage of revenue
- Customer acquisition cost trend
- Free cash flow and FCF margin
- Free cash flow per share
- Share-based compensation
- Diluted share count
- Regulatory exposure by product category
- Concentration risk from any single growth product

## Thesis Breakers

The tenbagger thesis weakens if:

- Growth depends on one temporary product category
- Customer acquisition costs rise faster than revenue
- Retention deteriorates
- Free cash flow per share fails to improve
- Dilution offsets operating progress
- Regulatory changes restrict major products
- The brand loses trust or faces quality issues
- Competitors copy the offering and compress margins
- Management over-expands into low-return categories

## Verdict

Watchlist / promising candidate.

HIMS has a plausible tenbagger setup if its vertical healthcare model continues scaling with durable retention, improving free cash flow per share, and controlled dilution. The core thesis is not simply revenue growth. The core thesis is whether scale turns into better customer value and stronger per-share economics.
